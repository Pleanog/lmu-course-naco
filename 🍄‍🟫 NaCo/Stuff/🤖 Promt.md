## Understanding User Needs using AI in Focus Groups

AI can be used to simulate and analyze user needs, acting as a complementary tool in traditional HCD approaches. This includes:
- **Generating synthetic responses** for surveys and interviews.
	- Since an Ai is just writing the "next logical word", the data is usable but also has a certain bias to it (from the data the LLM was trained on)
- **Simulating focus group discussions** to explore different user perspectives.
- **Automating transcription and summarization** of real user data.

> [!CITE] Reality and Software - Albrecht Schmidt  
> "Software is not just an interface; it is the real world in digital form."
#### Comparison of AI, Human, and Mixed Approaches in User Research

|Approach|Advantages|Disadvantages|Risks|
|---|---|---|---|
|**AI-based**|Fast data generation, scalable, cost-efficient|Lacks human intuition, may generate biased data|Synthetic responses may misrepresent real users|
|**Human-based**|Deep insights, emotional understanding, real feedback|Time-consuming, expensive, limited scalability|Subjectivity, small sample sizes|
|**Mixed**|Combines AI speed with human insight, balanced results|Requires careful integration and validation|Risk of over-reliance on AI-generated insights|

> [!TIP] Using AI for User Research  
> AI can enhance user research by **generating data at scale**, but human validation is essential to ensure relevance and accuracy. Biases and historical are part of LLMs so for highly new or risky context using AI can be outright problematic.

## **Optimization-Based Keyboard Design**


![[Pasted image 20250224120506.png]]

### **Why Optimize Keyboard Layouts?**

- QWERTY is **historically optimized for typewriters**, not touchscreens.
- Alternative layouts reduce **finger movement** and improve efficiency.

### **Optimization Strategies**

|Method|Description|
|---|---|
|Random Search|Generates random layouts and selects the best.|
|Simulated Annealing|Gradually refines layouts by accepting probabilistic changes.|
|Heuristic Methods|Use AI models to find the best design based on constraints.|
![[Pasted image 20250224120006.png]]

> [!question] Name and explain the key components of optimization-based UI design.  
> Key components include **objective functions** (define what is being optimized), **design constraints** (limit solutions to practical options), and **search algorithms** (find the best possible solution).
